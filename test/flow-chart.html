The Ocean Compass

Mystery of Captain HighTides. Players follow the trail of a legendary explorer whose mystical compass has vanished from the Nautical Museum.

They travel across the world’s oceans, finding learning items, compass pieces, mysterious clues, stickers and rewards.

Module architecture

This flow chart captures what needs to be produced for each part of the module.

Core loop:

Explore → find item → watch learning video → unlock sticker → read facts → play mini-game → upgrade sticker → complete collection.

Full learning outcomes

This module teaches children about the five oceans, exploration, navigation, marine ecosystems, ocean wildlife, polar environments, underwater archaeology, human impact on the sea, and famous ocean locations such as the Great Barrier Reef, the Arctic Ocean and the Titanic wreck site.

Science & nature

  • Marine animals and their adaptations
  • Habitats including reefs, lagoons, polar regions and deep sea
  • Environmental issues such as ocean litter

Geography & history

  • Major world oceans and regions
  • Navigation, maps, lighthouses and exploration
  • Shipwrecks, artefacts and underwater discovery
