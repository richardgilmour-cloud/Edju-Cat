Our approach

Edju Cat currently does not use analytics or advertising cookies. We use only limited browser storage and third-party technology needed to remember your choices, provide forms and protect those forms from abuse.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small files placed on your device by a website. Similar technologies, including local browser storage, can remember information about your visit. Some are essential for a service to work; others require your permission.

Your choices

You can clear or block cookies and browser storage through your browser settings. Doing so may cause the notice to reappear or prevent forms from working correctly. If we add optional analytics or advertising technology in future, we will ask for your permission before it loads.
